# Demo Hotel Web

A **responsive hotel website demo** showcasing rooms, events, dining, and amenities. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this project demonstrates a clean and modern hotel web design suitable for learning or portfolio purposes.

## 🛏️ Rooms & Suites

Explore the variety of accommodations offered:

- **Royal Rooms**: Spacious and elegantly designed for a premium experience.  
- **Deluxe Rooms**: Comfortable rooms equipped with modern amenities.  
- **Super Deluxe Rooms**: Premium rooms offering enhanced comfort and facilities.  
- **Family Rooms**: Spacious accommodations perfect for families.  

## 🎉 Events & Celebrations

Showcase of versatile venues suitable for different occasions:

- **Weddings**: Elegant setups for unforgettable moments.  
- **Receptions**: Customizable venues with beautiful decor.  
- **Birthdays**: Themed celebrations for joyful experiences.  
- **Anniversaries**: Personalized packages for special occasions.  

## 🍽️ Dining & Specialties

Demonstrates dining options and culinary offerings:

- **Lake Mocktail**: Refreshing beverages by a scenic location.  
- **Buffet**: A variety of dishes to satisfy all tastes.  
- **Dining**: Exquisite options for a delightful experience.  

## 🏞️ Amenities & Facilities

Highlights essential services and guest facilities:

- **Free Parking**: Ample parking space.  
- **24/7 Support**: Assistance available anytime.  
- **Event Hosting**: Flexible venues for different types of events.
